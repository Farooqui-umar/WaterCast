Privacy notice

How WaterCaste handles your information. Last updated: see the deployment's release notes.

What we collect

  • Help requests (SOS): the place you choose or share, its coordinates, the issue type, urgency and the description you type. Visitors only ever see the place name, issue type and status of a request - never coordinates or your description.
  • Community posts: the text, optional name and optional locality you type. Posts are public.
  • A random device ID stored in your browser (not linked to your identity) so we can limit spam and compute your Trust Score.
  • Your location - only if you allow your browser to share it, or type it yourself. It is used to fill your locality, show live weather for that spot and attach a position to a help request you send. It is kept in your browser session and is not stored on the server unless you send a help request.
  • Staff accounts (officials): username, password hash and a log of staff actions.

Who we share it with

To show weather and place names we send coordinates or the place name you searched to our data providers (the weather and geocoding services configured by the operator, by default Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap). We do not sell personal data and do not run advertising or third-party analytics.

Cookies and storage

A single strictly-necessary, HttpOnly session cookie is set only when a staff member signs in. Your browser's local storage keeps the random device ID; session storage keeps your typed location for the current visit.

How long we keep it

Requests, confirmations, alerts and posts older than the operator's retention period (default 365 days) are deleted automatically.

Your choices

You can refuse location access and type a place instead, clear your browser storage at any time, and ask the operator to delete a post or request you made.
